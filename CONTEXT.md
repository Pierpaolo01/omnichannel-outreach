# Outreach

Language for an internal product that coordinates business outreach and records sales conversations.

## Language

**Company**:
A business being considered or contacted, with its general contact details and associated decision makers.
_Avoid_: Using company and person interchangeably.

**Decision maker**:
A person associated with a company who can influence or decide whether to buy the offered service.
_Avoid_: Treating a company account or main phone number as a person's identity.

**Lead**:
A decision maker enrolled in a campaign, or a company enrolled through its company contact details when no decision maker is known. Each lead runs its own sequence; a decision maker's company groups them with colleagues and holds shared contact details.
_Avoid_: Calling a CSV row or an unenrolled contact a lead.

**Company contact details**:
A company's general main line and shared inbox, such as info@. Calls may use them for any lead. A decision maker's email and SMS go only to their own details; a company lead uses the company's.
_Avoid_: Treating a company main line or shared inbox as a person's own contact details.

**Campaign**:
A coordinated outreach effort containing imported leads, outreach strategies, cross-channel sequence and message copy.
_Avoid_: Assuming one campaign is limited to one channel.

**Channel track**:
A campaign's first message and follow-ups on one channel, sent in order with a wait before each follow-up.
_Avoid_: Calling one channel's follow-ups the sequence.

**Sequence**:
How a campaign arranges its channel tracks: which tracks start together and which start a set delay after another track finishes.
_Avoid_: Treating unrelated channel-specific schedules as a coordinated sequence.

**Win-back branch**:
An alternative set of channel tracks within a campaign that a lead enters after declining on a call, replacing the rest of their sequence, with no further cold call.
_Avoid_: Calling a separate follow-up campaign a win-back branch.

**Call task**:
A prospect phone call placed on the user's daily list for him to work through manually.
_Avoid_: Using call task to imply an autonomous AI voice conversation.

**Call outcome**:
What happened on one call task, logged by the user to complete it, such as no answer, callback requested or conversation.
_Avoid_: Using call outcome for the lead's outreach status.

**Callback**:
A call task at a time the prospect asked for, during which the lead's automated outreach is paused.
_Avoid_: Calling a user-chosen redial a callback.

**Redial**:
The user's choice to move an unreached call task onto the next day's dial list.
_Avoid_: Automatic retry.

**Outreach status**:
Where one lead's automated outreach stands in its campaign, such as queued, active, waiting on call, callback scheduled, paused, replied, in win-back, stopped or sequence finished. Each lead has exactly one.
_Avoid_: Confusing it with a call outcome.

**Reply handoff**:
The point where any reply from a lead, including an auto-reply or opt-out, stops that lead's automated outreach in the campaign. The user continues the conversation wherever the prospect replied, outside the product's outreach.
_Avoid_: Calling automated appointment booking, reply sorting or inbox management part of the reply handoff.

**Test run**:
A campaign's full sequence sent to test contacts from the real sender accounts, with each wait cut short, including test call tasks and a reply that stops it. It checks that everything arrives, before launch or after an edit.
_Avoid_: Calling a test run a launch, or a pre-launch preview.

**Test contact**:
The user's own email, mobile or social profile, chosen to receive a test run.
_Avoid_: Treating a test contact as a lead.

**Channel**:
A way to contact someone, such as email, a phone call, SMS, LinkedIn, Instagram or Facebook.
_Avoid_: Confusing a channel with a sender account.

**Sender account**:
The identity used to contact a prospect on a channel, such as one email inbox or social profile.
_Avoid_: Using account without distinguishing sender identity from a target company.

**Conversation**:
An exchange of messages with a person or business in a particular channel thread.
_Avoid_: Calling every cross-channel activity item the same conversation.

**CRM**:
The central record of companies, people, contact details, campaign activity, conversations and sales progress.
_Avoid_: Treating a sending provider's campaign copy as the complete customer record.

Source: product meanings established in the November 11, 2026 scoping conversation. Add or amend terms through subsequent product decisions.
