# Outreach

Language for an internal product that coordinates business outreach and records sales conversations.

## Language

**Company**:
A business being considered or contacted, with its general contact details and associated decision makers.
_Avoid_: Using company and person interchangeably.

**Decision maker**:
A person associated with a company who can influence or decide whether to buy the offered service.
_Avoid_: Treating a company account or main phone number as a person's identity.

**Campaign**:
A coordinated outreach effort containing imported leads, outreach strategies, cross-channel sequence and message copy.
_Avoid_: Assuming one campaign is limited to one channel.

**Sequence**:
The ordered outreach steps and conditions a campaign applies across its channels.
_Avoid_: Treating unrelated channel-specific schedules as a coordinated sequence.

**Call task**:
A prospect phone call placed on the user's daily list for him to work through manually.
_Avoid_: Using call task to imply an autonomous AI voice conversation.

**Call outcome**:
What happened on one call task, logged by the user to complete it, such as no answer, callback requested or conversation.
_Avoid_: Using call outcome for the lead's sales stage.

**Callback**:
A call task at a time the prospect asked for, during which the lead's automated outreach is paused.
_Avoid_: Calling a user-chosen redial a callback.

**Redial**:
The user's choice to move an unreached call task onto the next day's dial list.
_Avoid_: Automatic retry.

**Sales stage**:
Where a lead stands in the sales process, such as interested or appointment booked, recorded separately from any call outcome.
_Avoid_: Status, call result.

**Reply handoff**:
The point where a prospect's reply stops automated outreach and the user takes over the conversation to arrange an appointment.
_Avoid_: Calling automated appointment booking part of the reply handoff.

**Channel**:
A way to contact someone, such as email, a phone call, SMS, LinkedIn, Instagram or Facebook.
_Avoid_: Confusing a channel with a sender account.

**Sender account**:
The identity used to contact a prospect on a channel, such as one email inbox or social profile.
_Avoid_: Using account without distinguishing sender identity from a target company.

**Conversation**:
An exchange of messages with a person or business in a particular channel thread.
_Avoid_: Calling every cross-channel activity item the same conversation.

**CRM**:
The central record of companies, people, contact details, campaign activity, conversations and sales progress.
_Avoid_: Treating a sending provider's campaign copy as the complete customer record.

Source: product meanings established in the November 11, 2026 scoping conversation. Add or amend terms through subsequent product decisions.
